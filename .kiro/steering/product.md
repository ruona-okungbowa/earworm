# Product

Earworm turns messy human input (knocks, claps, hums, whistles, taps) into timestamped, testable events.

It is a Kiroween 2026 entry in the Skeleton Crew category: one kit, three apps in unrelated worlds, linked by "a rhythm is a message".

## Apps

- **Do As I Do**: séance horror. The user knocks on their desk.
- **Ditdah**: learn and send Morse by humming, whistling or the space bar.
- **Loophole**: clap a beat, it snaps to the grid, layer four parts, share by link.
